Privacy notice

In short: this site sets no cookies, runs no scripts, has no analytics and loads nothing from other servers. The only data processed is what your browser sends to fetch a page.

Who is responsible

The controller under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) is the person named in the legal notice, who can be reached at the contact details given there.

Hosting

nexumia.de is served by Cloudflare Workers, a service of Cloudflare, Inc., 101 Townsend St, San Francisco, CA 94107, USA. When you open a page, your browser sends the usual request data to Cloudflare’s network: your IP address, date and time, the address of the page or file, the referring page if your browser sends one, and your browser’s identification (user agent). Cloudflare needs this data to deliver the page and to protect its network against attacks.

The site has no server-side code of its own and its operator keeps no log files and no visitor statistics. Cloudflare processes the data on the operator’s behalf under a data processing agreement and, for the security of its network, also as a controller of its own; how long it keeps such data is described in Cloudflare’s privacy policy. Data may be processed in countries outside the European Union, including the USA. Cloudflare is certified under the EU–US Data Privacy Framework and its agreement includes the EU standard contractual clauses.

The legal basis is Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR: the legitimate interest in offering this site reliably and securely.

What this site does not do

  • No cookies and nothing else stored in your browser.
  • No JavaScript, no analytics, no tracking, no advertising.
  • No requests to other servers: fonts, images and styles come from nexumia.de itself.
  • No forms, accounts or newsletters.

Links to other sites

The site links to games.nexumia.de, flatpak.nexumia.de, GitHub and phosh.mobi. Nothing is sent to them until you follow a link. From then on that site’s own privacy information applies; games.nexumia.de and flatpak.nexumia.de are hosted differently from this site.

If you write an email

If you contact the operator by email, your address and message are used to answer you and are deleted when the matter is settled, unless the law requires keeping them (Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR).

Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right of access (Art. 15), rectification (Art. 16), erasure (Art. 17), restriction of processing (Art. 18) and data portability (Art. 20), and the right to object to processing that is based on legitimate interests (Art. 21). You can also lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority, for example the one where you live (Art. 77).
