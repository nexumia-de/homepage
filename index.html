Free apps for game nights and Linux phones.

Nexumia is a small, independent project. It makes party games that turn a TV and a few phones into a game table, and native apps for phones that run Linux. No accounts, no ads, no tracking.

Play the games See the phone apps

games.nexumia.de

Couch games in your browser

Put the game on the TV or a laptop, pick up your phones and play. The phones become controllers, score sheets and secret hands, and they connect to the big screen peer to peer, without a game server in between.

  • Runs in any modern browser; install it as an app if you like.
  • Works offline once loaded. Nothing to sign up for.
  • 15 games, in English and German.
Open the games

  • Dice and score sheets

    Classic dice games, dice bingo and a dart score keeper.

  • Party and deduction

    Werewolf, Imposter, drawing and guessing, and writing stories together.

  • Music

    A karaoke duel that scores your pitch, and sheet-music practice.

  • Cards and table

    A game table for card, board and dice games with hidden hands.

  • Puzzles

    A daily word puzzle and Sudoku, alone or as a duel.

Apps for Linux phones

Native GTK and libadwaita apps for phones running Phosh, such as the PinePhone or the Librem 5. They adapt to a desktop window just as well, and they install and update through GNOME Software.

Cirro

Rain radar with a 2-hour forecast

Cirro shows the rain radar for Germany on a map and animates it from the past hour to two hours ahead, so you can see when the shower reaches you. It uses the radar nowcast that the Deutscher Wetterdienst publishes as open data.

Cirro is not affiliated with the Deutscher Wetterdienst. Map data © OpenStreetMap contributors.

More apps are on the way. All of them will appear in the same repository: add it once and GNOME Software keeps every app up to date. 

flatpak remote-add --user --if-not-exists nexumia https://flatpak.nexumia.de/nexumia.flatpakrepo
